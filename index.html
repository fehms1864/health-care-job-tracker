How the filtering works · feed health
  • Where: UK and Netherlands roles at the UK/NL watchlist companies; Middle East roles (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon) at the Middle East companies.
  • Removed by title: engineering and technical roles, analytics and data roles, finance roles, clinical practitioner roles, Director/VP/Head-of level, internships, and clearly unrelated roles like sales reps or recruiters.
  • Removed by language: the posting is written in Dutch or Arabic, or says Dutch or Arabic is required. Roles where it's only "a plus" stay in.
  • Ranking: each role is scored on healthcare, strategy, operations, implementation and consulting words in the title and description. Title matches count double. "Best fit" means a score of 25 or more.
Job data comes from each employer's own job board and is refreshed once a day. Everything you type on this page stays on this device. Listings can be removed, mislabelled or missing, so always confirm the role, the language requirement and visa sponsorship directly with the employer.
